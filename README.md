# 🚕 Uber Ride Analytics Dashboard | Power BI Project

An interactive Power BI dashboard project built to analyze Uber ride data and uncover business insights related to bookings, revenue, ride performance, and customer behavior.

This project focuses on transforming raw ride data into meaningful insights using data visualization and business analysis techniques.

---

# 🚀 Project Overview

The goal of this project was to:
- Analyze ride booking performance
- Identify revenue trends
- Understand customer behavior
- Detect operational issues
- Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

The dashboard provides insights into:
- Ride completion rates
- Lost bookings
- Revenue generation
- Vehicle performance
- Customer contribution
- Operational inefficiencies

---

---

# 🚨 Major Business Insight

## ⚠️ Nearly 38% of bookings are being lost

This was the most critical insight discovered during the analysis.

Possible reasons include:
- Driver cancellations
- Long waiting times
- Surge pricing
- App performance issues

Even recovering a small percentage of these lost bookings could significantly increase overall revenue.

---

# 🚗 Revenue Analysis by Vehicle Type

### Revenue Performance Ranking
🥇 Auto → Highest Revenue  
🥈 Bike → Strong Performance  
🥉 Go Mini / Go Sedan → Moderate Demand  
📉 Uber XL → Lowest Revenue  

### Key Insight
Affordable transportation options dominate customer demand.

### Suggested Business Strategy
- Expand Auto & Bike availability
- Improve pricing strategy for premium rides
- Optimize Uber XL demand management

---

# 👥 Customer Revenue Insights

### Findings
- Top customers generate around ₹4K–₹4.5K each
- Revenue distribution is balanced across users
- Business is not dependent on a small customer group

### Opportunities Identified
- Loyalty Programs
- Ride Subscriptions
- Frequent Rider Rewards
- Personalized Offers
